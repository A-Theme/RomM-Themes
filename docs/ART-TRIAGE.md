## What the verdicts mean

- **good** - drop it in. Sample the palette, crop, check contrast, pick an effect.
- **workable** - usable, but it needs work the notes name. Worth it for art that
  earns it; not worth it for a picture that is merely fine.
- **reject** - the shape or the content fights the screen. Portrait art is the
  usual case: there is no crop to 16:9 that keeps the subject.

Nothing here is a judgement on the art, only on how it behaves as a 1280x720
background behind a UI.
